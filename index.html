Research Methodology

This study evaluates the accuracy of AI-based automated measurements on 3D digital upper arch models compared to gold-standard manual measurements.

🦷

3D Digital Models

Upper arch dental models obtained via intraoral scanning and exported as STL files for digital analysis.

🤖

AI Landmark Detection

Deep learning model automatically identifies individual tooth landmarks and measures mesiodistal widths from STL geometry.

📐

Arch Measurements

Inter-canine width, inter-molar width, and arch shape classification are computed from AI-detected landmarks.

📊

Index Calculations

Pont's Index, Crowding Index, Ashley & Huse Index, and Incisor Height Index derived from AI measurements.

✅

Manual Validation

All AI measurements validated against manual measurements by an experienced orthodontist using digital calipers.

📈

Statistical Analysis

Mean absolute deviation, intraclass correlation, and Bland–Altman analysis assess agreement between methods.

Measurement Indices Used

Index Formula Clinical Significance
Pont's Index Sum of 4 upper incisors / arch width × 100 Predicts required arch width for alignment
Crowding Index Arch length required − Arch length available Quantifies degree of dental crowding (mm)
Ashley & Huse Index Canine fossa width × 100 / TM Arch depth to width ratio assessment
Incisor Height Index Incisor height / inter-canine width × 100 Vertical-horizontal proportion of anterior teeth
Tanaka & Johnston ½ mandibular incisor width + 10.5 (canine) / 11.0 (premolar) Mixed dentition analysis for space prediction
